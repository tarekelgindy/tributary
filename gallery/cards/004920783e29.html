The actual points of disagreement

  • Whether Macklemore's speech constituted political advocacy or hate speech / antisemitic rhetoric

  • Whether Ed Sheeran's 'apolitical' stance is a neutral position or itself a political choice that enables harm
  • Whether artist solidarity withdrawals were principled moral witness or performative activism
  • Whether publicly subsidized venues have different First Amendment obligations than private ones, and if so, what they are
