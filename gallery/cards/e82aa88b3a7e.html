Common ground — verified shared facts

  • A financial disclosure document reported that Donald Trump received over $1 billion in income or value from cryptocurrency-related ventures.
  • Trump's financial disclosure was 927 pages in length.
  • The $TRUMP token experienced a significant price decline from its peak.
  • Trump pardoned Changpeng Zhao (CZ), founder of Binance.
  • Trump disclosed a financial interest in World Liberty Financial (WLF).

Repeated by all sides, unverified

  • The UAE sovereign wealth fund invested $500 million in World Liberty Financial.
  • Trump earned $636 million specifically from the $TRUMP token.
  • 988,905 retail wallets holding $TRUMP experienced losses.
  • Pakistan's government signed an MOU with World Liberty Financial.
  • The $TRUMP token's tokenomics allocate 80% of supply to Trump entities and 20% to the public.
  • Obama's final financial disclosure was 8 pages; Biden's was 11 pages.
  • Trump's disclosure filing incurred late fees and required a 45-day extension.

The actual points of disagreement

  • Whether Trump's crypto income constitutes a conflict of interest or self-dealing (Corruption framing) vs. whether it is legally permissible and reflects business success (Presidential Success framing)
  • Whether foreign investment in Trump crypto ventures represents a national security threat and pay-to-play channel (Foreign Influence framing) vs. whether outside fund managers handle his assets independently
  • Whether the $TRUMP token's structure and collapse caused systematic harm to retail investors (Retail Investor Harm framing) vs. whether retail participation in meme coins is a voluntary, speculative choice
  • Whether the 927-page disclosure represents unprecedented transparency (Presidential Transparency framing) or a document so vast it obscures rather than clarifies (Legislative Capture framing)
  • Whether Trump's crypto policy (GENIUS Act, deregulation) is a quid pro quo for personal enrichment (Corruption framing) vs. whether it reflects a coherent 'America First' crypto agenda (Presidential Success framing)
  • Whether the timing of the disclosure during Senate crypto bill negotiations poisoned legislative integrity (Legislative Capture framing) vs. whether it is irrelevant to the merits of crypto policy
  • Whether Trump's crypto dealings are politically reckless and will damage the GOP (Conservative Institutional Alarm framing) vs. whether they are legally sound and politically defensible
  • Whether meme coin tokenomics expose a regulatory gap that allows insider profit-taking (Crypto Market Structure framing) vs. whether meme coins are inherently speculative and require no special regulation
