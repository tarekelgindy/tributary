Concept A · The facet wheel

Common-ground pool at the center (two strata again); framings as spokes. Each facet carries one teal "sees" line and one rust "misses" line. The dashed ring says they orbit the same event. Best as the shareable figure — one glance: same water, five lenses, every lens partial.

Concept B · The sees / misses ladder

One row per framing: what it sees (teal), what it misses (rust), and — the real edge data — a chip naming which framing carries that missing piece. Most informative; reads top to bottom; best on the share page and viewer rather than the card.
