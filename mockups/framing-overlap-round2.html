Framing overlap, round 2 — visual-first, color-coded

Each framing gets its own color (a code that could then thread through the whole page: delta channels, framing cells, carrier chips). Text reduced to names + one micro-label. Same honest base: the center/station/core is the recorded shared foundation; the tips are what only each lens stresses.

Concept D · The Venn rosette

Five translucent petals, one per framing, all overlapping at the shared center — the literal Venn read: everything meets in the middle, each petal reaches where only it looks. Petal-tip micro-labels are each framing's leading emphasis. Closest to what you described.

Concept E · The subway fan

One shared station, five colored lines leaving it. The name rides inside the band; the terminus label is that line's distinctive territory. Boldest and simplest; scales to 6–8 lines better than the rosette; instantly color-codes the rest of the page.

Concept F · The chromatic dial

A segmented ring around the shared core — the most compact and icon-like, with a legend carrying one "sees" and one "misses" micro-line per color. The dial itself stays nearly wordless; the legend does the explaining.
